# Ep 17 - AI wildlife chase documentaries (1.2M views style)

The recipe behind "Snow Leopard Hunts a Mother Mountain Goat and Her Baby on a Deadly Cliff": one predator, a mother
and her young, a chase on a cliff, a near fall, told by one calm narrator over photoreal AI clips. We make the same
story with the right animals (mountain goats live only in North America, so our prey is the Siberian ibex).

## Files
| Step | File | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [01_wildlife_chase_writer.md](01_wildlife_chase_writer.md) | System prompt: real animals first, the chase shape, animal sheets with sizes, empty places, about 40 clips with a length per action, an edit map. |
| 2 | [02_voice_components_clips_and_edit.md](02_voice_components_clips_and_edit.md) | Every image-page and video-page setting with the reason, the narration-first edit, the problems we hit and the fixes. |

## The rest of the recipe
3. One picture run: three animal sheets (four views each, size in centimetres) and nine empty places.
4. Clips from components: Components tab, Omni, 720p, [4s] / [6s] / [8s] / [10s] by the action.
5. Narration first, clips under each line in shots of about 3 seconds, wild sound low, a soft music bed.

## What we measured in the original
- 10:14, 1.19M views; one narrator, about 130 words a minute, music and wind underneath; the chase is the first 6:35.
- The channel: 25 videos, almost all predator vs mountain goat; the typical one about 22,000 views.

Example from the video: a snow leopard, a mother ibex and her kid (38 clips, 2:38).
