# Ep 05 - Easy English listening stories (A2) with a cartoon family

The recipe behind animated "Easy English Listening" videos for learners: a tiny story engine (a child's everyday
problem, a playful plan, one moral line), a fixed cartoon family with slow, clear voices, prompts that leave room for
a silent reaction after every line, and an edit that talks, then shows.

## Files
| Step | File | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [01_easy_english_story_writer.md](01_easy_english_story_writer.md) | The story engine, the A1-A2 language rules, and the two clip types: LINE clips (one short line, then silent reaction) and BEAT clips (no words). Shot-list format with headcount, blocking, camera and duration tags. |
| 2 | [02_character_and_location_prompts.md](02_character_and_location_prompts.md) | 4-view cartoon character sheets, empty locations, slow-and-clear voice descriptions, clip settings, the "talk, then show" edit, and the problems we hit with their fixes. |

## The rest of the recipe
3. Put every character in a character library with its sheet and a saved custom voice, and make sure the library
   switch is ON before you run a batch.
4. Video from ingredients (Omni / Veo components), 720p, duration tags: `[6s]` for a line clip, `[4s]` for a beat clip.
5. Watch every clip. Fix a bad row on the same table and retry that row only.
6. Edit: start 0.4 s before the first word and keep about 2.5 s after the last one; put beat clips between lines
   (about 20 cuts a minute, about 45 spoken words a minute). One caption per line, the whole sentence, held until
   the next line. Light, playful music under everything.

## What we measured in the original
- A 43-minute video made of about 12 short skits; the title sells only the first one.
- Speech fills only 28% of the runtime (a dialogue drama: 43%), about 53 words a minute, with a median pause of
  2.6 s between lines - but 22 cuts a minute, and nearly half of the shots have no words at all.

Example from the video: "Mia Is Too Shy to Talk at Grandpa's Bakery... Then Grandpa Has a CLEVER Plan!"
(4 characters, 57 clips, 3:35).
