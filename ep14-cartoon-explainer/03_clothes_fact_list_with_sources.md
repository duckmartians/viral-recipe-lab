Every claim we narrate, graded and sourced. C = confirmed, R = a range or estimate, X = could not confirm (not narrated).


Grades: C = confirmed by a reliable source (exact figure given). R = reported or estimated, with a range. X = could not confirm, or contradicted.

## Where the original rounds or overstates

- Row 7: "In 2003 ... about 107,000 years". The paper's own range is about 32,000-112,000 years (clade about 72,000). 107,000 is from a press release.
- Row 8: the study is dated 2010 online / 2011 in print. The range is correct. Do not shorten to "170,000".
- Row 11: 1.2 million years is an upper age for the gene change, not a fur-loss date.
- Row 13: "more than almost any other mammal" is not sourced. The source compares humans to chimps and macaques (10 times).
- Row 22: "Contre Bandier's" should be Contrebandiers.
- Row 25: "oldest known needle" is about 50,000 years per the excavator only. Chronology papers give about 40,000.
- Row 32: shells on a cape, days-long dyes, restricted furs are not tied to any cited find.
- Row 34: "toddlers don't have it" is not supported.

## Do not say

1. "No other animal gets dressed" as a flat fact (row 2). Use "no other animal makes clothes".
2. "Clothes began 170,000 years ago" or "107,000 years ago" as a single number (rows 7, 8). Always give the range.
3. "We lost our fur 1.2 million years ago" (row 11). Say "may be as old as 1.2 million years".
4. "Humans sweat more than almost any other mammal" (row 13). Say "far more than other apes".
5. "Proof of the first clothes" at Contrebandiers Cave (row 21). Say "tools and bones that point to making clothing".
6. "Neanderthals only wore capes" (row 23). It is a debated idea.
7. "The needle won the Ice Age" or "needles caused Neanderthal extinction" (row 27).
8. "Oldest needle is exactly 50,000 years" and the Sibudu 61,000 needle (row 25). Say "about 40,000 to 50,000 years".
9. Ötzi's "grass coat or cape" (row 30). Museum says mat or backpack part. Check the "bearskin soles" wording against the museum shoe page before using it (row 29).
10. "Toddlers feel no shame about nakedness" and any claim that nudity shame is innate (rows 34, 35).
11. Shells sewn on capes, days-long dyes, restricted furs as facts (row 32), unless a specific find is cited.
12. "Contre Bandier's cave". Write "Contrebandiers Cave" or just "Smugglers' Cave".
