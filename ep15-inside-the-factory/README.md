# Ep 15 - AI "inside the factory" videos (1M views style)

The recipe behind a 17-minute "how is gochujang chili paste made? (full process)" video: one product followed station
by station, from the truck of fresh chilies to the truck leaving with pallets, told only by photoreal AI clips of
machines with their own sound (no narrator, no music). We make the same factory with AI, with unbranded machines
and labels with no words.

## Files
| Step | File | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [01_factory_process_writer.md](01_factory_process_writer.md) | System prompt: the five parts of the factory, the tag rules, one empty picture per station, the ingredient and product sheets, 30 clip prompts of 8 seconds. |
| 2 | [02_stations_clips_and_edit.md](02_stations_clips_and_edit.md) | Every image-page and video-page setting with the reason, the edit, the problems we hit and the fixes. |

## The rest of the recipe
3. All pictures in one run: save folder = components folder, no sub-folders, five rows at once, wait time immediately.
4. One empty picture per station (we made 18): every clip tags one, so the AI never invents the machine.
5. Clips from components: Components tab, Omni, 720p, every prompt starts with [8s] (the original is 8-second clips).
6. Edit: whole clips in factory order, hard cuts, the clips' own sound, no music; trim only a clip that jumps elsewhere.
