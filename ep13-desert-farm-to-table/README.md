# Ep 13 - AI farm to table videos (10M views style)

The recipe behind "Growing White Mulberries in the Desert": one crop followed from barren desert land to a jar of jam
at home, told only by photoreal AI clips with their own sound (no narrator, no music). We make the same story with AI,
with an invented store, unbranded machines and labels with no words.

## Files
| Step | File | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [01_farm_to_table_writer.md](01_farm_to_table_writer.md) | System prompt: the journey, the tag rules, one place picture per stage, the fruit and product sheets, clip prompts with a length per action. |
| 2 | [02_places_clips_and_edit.md](02_places_clips_and_edit.md) | Every image-page and video-page setting with the reason, the edit, the problems we hit and the fixes. |

## The rest of the recipe
3. All pictures in one run: save folder = components folder, no sub-folders, five rows at once, wait time immediately.
4. One place picture per stage (we made 20): every clip tags one, so the AI never invents the farm.
5. Clips from components: Components tab, Omni, 720p, duration by the [Ns] tag: [4s] / [6s] / [8s] / [10s] by the action.
6. Edit: whole clips in story order, hard cuts, the clips' own sound, no music.

## What we measured in the original
- 15:01, 10.4M views; 131 shots, median 7.2 s, 2-12 s; no narrator, no music; 26 short worker lines.
- The channel: 213 videos with the same idea; median about 3,300 views, 6 over 1M, this one is the only one over 10M.
