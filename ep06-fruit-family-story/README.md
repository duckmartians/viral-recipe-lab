# Ep 06 - Fruit-head family stories (Slow English cartoon)

The recipe behind fruit-head family cartoons: the same story engine as the big Slow English channels, a family with
fruit for heads that viewers recognise in one frame, two-word lines followed by the picture that carries the story,
and a mess that stays consistent from clip to clip.

| 2 | [02_fruit_character_sheets_and_edit.md](02_fruit_character_sheets_and_edit.md) | Fruit-head character sheets, a clean and a messy location picture, slow-and-clear voices, clip settings, the line-then-picture edit, and the problems we hit with their fixes. |

## The rest of the recipe
3. Put every character in a character library with its sheet and a saved voice, and switch the library ON.
4. Video from ingredients (Omni / Veo components), 720p, duration tags `[6s]` for a line clip, `[4s]` for a picture
   clip, the folder of location pictures set in the advanced settings.
5. Check every clip frame by frame. Fix a bad row on the same table and retry that row only.
6. Edit: start just before the line, keep about 2.5 s of the face, then cut to the picture. One short caption per
   line. Light, bouncy music under the clip sound.

## What we measured in the original
- 15:26, three skits; 825K views in 8 days on a channel with 7.4K subscribers.
- Characters talk only 17% of the time, 2 words per line; about 15 cuts a minute; after every line the cut goes to a
  reveal of the mess, a shocked face, or a prop insert.

Example from the video: "Kiko Made a Flour Mess... Can Rubi Fix It Before Mom Comes Home?" (3 characters, 42 clips, 3:08).
