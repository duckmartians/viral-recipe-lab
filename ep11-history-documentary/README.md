# Ep 11 - AI history documentary videos (2.7M views style)

The recipe behind "How a 3,000-Year-Old Obsession Built the Suez Canal": an animated history documentary with one
calm narrator over fast anime graphic-novel shots. We make the same story with AI, with a source for every fact.

## Files
| Step | File | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [01_history_docu_writer.md](01_history_docu_writer.md) | System prompt: facts first, the story shape, the narration, character sheets, empty places, clip prompts with the year in words. |
| 2 | [02_voice_components_clips_and_edit.md](02_voice_components_clips_and_edit.md) | The voice, every image-page and video-page setting with the reason, the narration-first edit, the problems we hit and the fixes. |
| 3 | [03_suez_fact_list_with_sources.md](03_suez_fact_list_with_sources.md) | Our 16 facts with sources, and the lines of the original we dropped. |

## The rest of the recipe
4. The voice: one file per narration line, one calm voice at normal speed.
5. The pictures: save folder = components folder, no sub-folders, five rows at once: a sheet per person and machine, every place empty.
6. Clips from components: Components tab, Omni, 720p, duration by the [8s] tag; test three clips first.
7. Edit: voice lines first, then each line's clips cut into ~2.5 s shots, quiet music under the voice.

## What we measured in the original
- 11:00, 2.78M views; 203 shots, median 2.1 s; one narrator, 1,536 words, about 140 words a minute.
- The channel: 43 videos about how famous projects were built; median about 61K views, 5 over 1M.

Example from the video: "The Canal the Desert Kept Taking Back" (same story, made with AI, 31 clips, 49 shots, 2:20).
