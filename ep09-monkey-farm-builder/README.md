# Ep 09 - AI baby monkey farm videos (100M views style)

The recipe behind the baby monkey farm video with 100 million views: a cute monkey with a colourful toy digger fixes
a farm and builds a little house for every rescued animal. 8-second clips, about ten short lines in eight minutes,
the clips' own sound everywhere else.

## Files
| Step | File | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [01_baby_animal_farm_builder.md](01_baby_animal_farm_builder.md) | System prompt: the story shape, the tag rules (3-letter prefixes, tag + dots) and the picture and clip prompts, section by section. |
| 2 | [02_picture_chain_clips_and_join.md](02_picture_chain_clips_and_join.md) | The picture chain (output folder = reference folder), clips from finished pictures, the join, and the problems we hit with their fixes. |

## The rest of the recipe
3. Set the image tool's output folder and reference folder to the same folder (no sub-folder per task).
4. Make the pictures in order: components, houses (tagging the farm), one middle picture, the first frames.
5. Clips with "Frames to Video": one tag per clip (its first frame), `[8s]`, 5 at a time, no delay.
6. Watch every clip in motion; repeat the hero's look and the house colours in words, also after a cut.
7. Join the clips back to back, keep the clip sound, no music. Next episode: same hero, same farm, a new job.

## What we measured in the original
- 8:18, 100M views; 62 clips, 49 of them exactly 8.0 s; one or two shots inside a clip.
- About ten short spoken lines; the rest is engine, hammer, peeps and bleats, different in every clip.
- The channel made truck and tractor animations before; three monkey-farm videos in seven days: 7.4M, 3.6M, 100M.

Example from the video: "Momo Rebuilds the Farm" (same story as the original, our own pictures and sound, 24 clips, 3:12).
