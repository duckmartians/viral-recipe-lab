# Ep 12 - AI diecast car showcase videos (3M views style)

The recipe behind "Mahindra Scorpio Diecast Models - Customised Indian Army & Indian Air Force Editions": two small
model SUVs in two liveries, filmed at ground level on moss, a dirt track, a rock and a muddy puddle, music only. The
original is filmed with real die-cast models; we make the same video with AI, with an invented car and invented
liveries (no real brand, badge or insignia).

## Files
| Step | File | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [01_diecast_showcase_writer.md](01_diecast_showcase_writer.md) | System prompt: the video shape, the tag rules, the car pictures, model sheets and empty places, clip prompts with camera height. |
| 2 | [02_components_clips_and_edit.md](02_components_clips_and_edit.md) | Every image-page and video-page setting with the reason, the edit, the problems we hit and the fixes. |

## The rest of the recipe
3. The pictures: save folder = components folder, no sub-folders, five rows at once, wait time immediately.
4. Model sheets: each one tags its car picture and shows the car from six sides; every clip tags the sheet, never the single picture.
5. Clips from components: Components tab, Omni, 720p, duration by the [8s] tag, a model sheet + one place per clip.
6. Edit: 20 clips into 30 short shots (start and end of a clip placed far apart), upbeat music under everything.

## What we measured in the original
- 2:19, 3.07M views; 31 shots, median 3.4 s, longest 9.1 s; music only, no voice.
- The channel: 325 videos with nearly the same title; median about 67K views, 35 over 1M.

Example from the video: "Two Tiny SUVs, One Forest Floor" (same idea, made with AI, 20 clips, 30 shots, 1:52).
