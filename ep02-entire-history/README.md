# Ep 02 - "The ENTIRE History of X in N Minutes"

The recipe behind stick-figure history epics: one idea that keeps repeating, a new cast in costumes for every
era, and a new still image about every 2.6 seconds.

## Files
| Step | File | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [01_scriptwriter_system_prompt.md](01_scriptwriter_system_prompt.md) | Finds the one pattern that repeats through a place's history, then writes the whole script around it (cold open, the loop, the late payoff), as a table: line + shot type + visual idea. |
| 2 | [02_script_to_image_prompts.md](02_script_to_image_prompts.md) | Locks the style, builds a CAST table (one costume line per leader and army), and turns every line into an image prompt with a template per shot type. |

## The rest of the recipe
3. Check every date, name and number the AI wrote before you record. In our sample, 3 of 18 lines needed fixing.
4. Generate one image per subtitle line. Reference images for the leaders who come back keep them identical.
5. Voice: any text-to-speech voice, fast: the original runs at about 170 words per minute. Raise the speed if needed.
6. Timing (SRT): a free way is CapCut desktop: voice on the timeline, Auto captions, proofread, export as SRT.
7. Pacing: a new picture about every 2.6 seconds (median of 239 shots), faster in the first minute, no motion.
8. Assemble: one image per subtitle line, hard cuts. Music: an instrumental with a little drama, kept low.

## Packaging (what the original channel repeats)
- Title: "The ENTIRE History of [place] in [N] Minutes".
- Thumbnail: one landmark illustration on the left, the place name in huge letters on the right, the years under it.

Example from the video: "The ENTIRE History of Venice" (cold open, 41 images).
