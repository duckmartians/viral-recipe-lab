# Ep 07 - Wordless animal short films (baby animal, no dialogue)

The recipe behind wordless baby-animal shorts: one body part the baby can't control yet (it causes every gag and
saves the day at the end), no words at all, sound that never stops, and comedy first, then the brave moment.

## Files
| Step | File | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [01_wordless_animal_short_writer.md](01_wordless_animal_short_writer.md) | The story engine, the gag grammar, the clip format (blocking, camera, action, SOUND, no words), light per mood, the tag rules, and "continue" for the next clips. |
| 2 | [02_animal_sheets_places_and_edit.md](02_animal_sheets_places_and_edit.md) | Animal character sheets, one location picture per place and light, voices that make sounds, clip settings, the fast-gags-slow-faces edit, and the problems we hit with their fixes. |

## The rest of the recipe
3. Put every animal in a character library with its sheet and a voice that makes sounds, and switch the library ON.
4. Video from ingredients (Omni / Veo components), 720p, duration tags `[4s]` for a gag, `[6s]` for emotion, the folder
   of location pictures set in the advanced settings. Pilot the hardest clips first.
5. Check every clip frame by frame. Fix a bad row on the same table and retry that row only.
6. Edit: about 2.5 s per gag clip, 4-5 s for faces and the ending. Keep the clip sounds, gentle music underneath.

## What we measured in the original
- 8:16, 1.6M views in 5 days on a channel with 8.4K subscribers; the same channel's narrated documentaries got a few
  thousand views.
- One spoken line in 8 minutes; music and effects silent for only about 68 s; voice sounds in about half the film;
  about 20 cuts a minute; about two thirds comedy.

Example from the video: "One Tall Neck" (4 animals, 42 clips, 2:13, not one word).
