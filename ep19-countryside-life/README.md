# Ep 19 - AI "peaceful countryside life" films (921K views style)

The recipe behind "Life in the countryside garden - bell pepper": one mountain village, one family, lots of animals,
food from the garden to the table, and no voice. Every shot is an AI clip of 8 seconds or less, cut to about 5.5
seconds, with the clip's own sound and a soft piano. We make the same kind of film with our own pictures and clips.

## Files
| Step | File | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [01_countryside_life_writer.md](01_countryside_life_writer.md) | System prompt: the shot list from dawn to night, base sheets and master pictures, outfits and corners drawn from them, first frames for crowded shots, clip prompts with a sound line, and the tag rules. |
| 2 | [02_places_outfits_clips_edit.md](02_places_outfits_clips_edit.md) | Every image-page, video-page and Auto Video Assembly setting with the reason, and the problems we hit with the fixes. |

## The rest of the recipe
3. Run 1: base sheets in plain clothes, the animals, one master picture of the village and one of the kitchen.
4. Run 2: outfits as edits of the base (the face stays), corners that tag the master (the village stays).
5. Run 3: first frames on the image page (up to 10 references) for shots that need more references than Omni takes
   (3; Omni Flash takes 7).
6. Clips in 1080p (tick 1080p before unticking 720p): crowded shots in Frames to Video, the rest in Components (Omni).
7. Edit: trim each clip to about 5.5 s, cut fly-ins and cuts inside clips, a soft piano under the clips' own sound.

## What we measured in the original
- 19:49, 921K views; 214 moving clips, median 5.6 s, none longer than 8 s; no speech; a soft piano under every shot.
- The channel: 74 videos, typical about 149K views, 52 of 74 over 100K. A format that repeats, not one lucky video.
