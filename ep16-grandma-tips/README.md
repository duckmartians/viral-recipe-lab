# Ep 16 - AI "grandma's tips" videos (576K views style)

The recipe behind "9 Ways I Keep My Bed Warm With No Heat": an older woman who lives alone talks to the camera from
her kitchen table, cut with warm photos of every tip and a numbered countdown. Every face and photo is AI. We make the
same video with our own character, safe and sourced tips, and edit pictures and talking clips together in one slideshow.

## Files
| Step | File | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [01_grandma_tips_writer.md](01_grandma_tips_writer.md) | System prompt: safe sourced tips, the countdown shape, one line = one picture or one talking clip = one subtitle, line tags l01.. in every file name, her sheet, the rooms, picture and clip prompts. |
| 2 | [02_pictures_clips_voice_slideshow.md](02_pictures_clips_voice_slideshow.md) | Every image-page, video-page (voice) and slideshow setting with the reason, and the problems we hit with the fixes. |

## The rest of the recipe
3. Pictures in two runs: her sheet and the rooms first, then one photo per line, each tagging her sheet and the room.
4. Talking clips: Components tab, Omni, a Voice Component, one clip per talking line, the exact words in quotes.
5. Narration in the same voice, one subtitle line per script line.
6. Slideshow: pictures and clips by their line tag; each clip plays whole with its own voice, the photos drift slowly.

## What we measured in the original
- 9:56, 576K views; 157 shots, median 3.3 s; she talks to the camera 34% of the time (shots of 7-9 s); one voice.
- The channel: 27 videos in about five weeks; the typical one about 1,600 views. An outlier.

Example from the video: "5 Ways I Keep My Bed Warm With No Heat" (our character Ruth, 38 lines, 3:53).
