# Ep 18 - AI "What happens if you..." cartoon explainers (1.5M views style)

The recipe behind "What Happens If You Trap a Spider and Forget About It?": something the viewer has done to a bug at
home, answered with real studies, one flat cartoon drawing for every line, and one calm voice with no music. Every
drawing and the voice are AI. We make the same video with our own drawings, clone the voice in Voice Studio, and edit
by subtitle line.

## The rest of the recipe
3. Drawings in two runs: the animal sheet, the person sheet and the room first, then one drawing per line.
4. Redo only the bad rows: ask for ONE single figure, not a sheet; the prompt's label is not part of the picture.
5. Voice: clone a voice you have the right to use (clean sample, Pick best part, AI suggest, save), then read the
   whole script with split mode Newline and export one MP3 plus the subtitle file.
6. Slideshow: drawings by their line tag, the subtitle file as the schedule, a slow pan and zoom on every drawing.

## What we measured in the original
- 3:46, 1.5M views; 79 shots, median 2.7 s, 68 of them still drawings; one voice at about 195 words a minute; no music.
- The channel: 22 videos, typical about 4,100 views; all 6 videos over 100K are about something YOU did to a bug.

Example from the video: "What Happens If You Trap a Spider and Forget About It?" (43 drawings, a cloned voice, 1:35).

Only clone a voice you have the right to use: your own, or one you have clear permission for.
